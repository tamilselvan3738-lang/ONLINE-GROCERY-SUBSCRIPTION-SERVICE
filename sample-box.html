Ideal for Everyday Cooking

The Everyday Cooking Vegetable Box

Curated for households cooking at home regularly. Contains robust staples, tender salad greens, aromatic base roots, and seasonal picks that support standard recipe preparation.

Leafy Greens (Spinach, Kale) 2 Bunches
Roots (Carrots, Red Onion) 1.5 kg
Base Crops (Tomatoes, Peppers) 1 kg
Culinary Herbs (Thyme, Basil) 2 Packs

Best Suited For: Meal-prepping couples and cooking enthusiasts seeking fresh organic greens.

Snacks and Breakfasts

The Fresh Orchard Fruit Box

Curated for natural snacking, breakfast toppings, and smoothie lovers. Packed with sweet orchard stone fruits, vitamin-rich citrus, and seasonal field berries harvested ripe.

Morning Citrus (Lemons, Oranges) 6 Units
Orchard Picks (Apples, Pears) 8 Units
Sweet Berries (Strawberries, Blueberries) 2 Packs
Energy Fruit (Bananas, Grapes) 1 kg

Best Suited For: Active families with school-going kids and healthy snack enthusiasts.

Pantry Stocking staples

The Curated Pantry Staples Box

Maintains your collection of dry kitchen basics. Never run out of foundational grains, small-batch dry pastas, high-protein nuts, and premium oils.

Organic Grains (Quinoa, Oats, Rice) 3 Jars
Artisanal Pastas (Penne, Fusilli) 2 Packs
Raw Nuts (Walnuts, Almonds) 500 g
Cold-Pressed Olive Oil 500 ml

Best Suited For: Households aiming to bypass dry food supermarkets and maintain an organized pantry.

Family Box vs Premium Crate

Compare our large-volume household boxes next to our specialized, curated gourmet creations.

Family Favorite High Volume

The Family Crate

Contains larger quantities of everyday vegetables (root crops, greens), citrus fruits, fresh berries, farm-fresh organic milk, and raw eggs. Supports multiple cooking events and breakfast routines.

Curated Selection Artisanal Specialties

The Premium Crate

Curated for gourmands. Includes small-batch artisanal cheeses, fresh herbs (rosemary, sage), heritage seasonal apples/figs, freshly baked organic sourdough bread, and estate cold-pressed oils.

Evolving With the Seasons

Supermarkets store crops in refrigerators for weeks to keep them uniform. SproutSub does the opposite. We align your boxes with seasonal crop cycles to keep your diet healthy, natural, and exciting.

Spring: Fresh peas, radishes, baby lettuce
Summer: Sweet peaches, corn, heirloom tomatoes
Autumn: Apples, pumpkins, sweet potatoes
Winter: Crisp kale, citrus, winter squash
