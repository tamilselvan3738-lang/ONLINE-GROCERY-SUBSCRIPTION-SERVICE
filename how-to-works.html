Start With the Subscription That Fits You.

Every home cooks, snacks, and plans meals differently. We begin by helping you match a subscription plan to your weekly household consumption. Whether it's produce-only boxes or curated pantry boxes, you set the base layout of what enters your kitchen.

Step 2: Frequency Scheduling

Set Your Delivery Rhythm

Match the delivery frequency to your grocery habits. Weekly schedules ensure a continuous stream of delicate greens and fruits, while biweekly frequencies are perfect for planning larger intervals and stocking dry staples.

Weekly Delivery Route

Crates arrive every Tuesday afternoon. Best for standard family meals.

Biweekly Delivery Route

Crates arrive alternate Thursdays. Best for low waste and meal preppers.

Step 6: Confirm and Deliver

From Confirmation to Doorstep

Once you lock in your weekly selections, our automated timeline takes care of the rest. We coordinate harvest and delivery paths to guarantee fresh, cold-insulated transit straight to your doorstep.

1. Wednesday Midnight

Customization deadline closes. We tally household boxes and send harvest lists directly to farm partners.

2. Friday Morning

Crops are harvested at sunrise, graded, and packed inside thermal fiber-insulated crates.

3. Tuesday Afternoon

Carbon-neutral delivery trucks arrive at your doorstep, dropping off your crate and collecting previous empty boxes.
