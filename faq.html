Subscription Questions
SproutSub offers five curated grocery crates: the Vegetable Box, the Fruit Box, the Pantry Staples Box, the Family Grocery Box, and the Premium Grocery Box. Each represents a distinct crop composition scaled to match specific kitchen consumption habits.
Yes, all vegetables, fruits, and pantry grains included in our crates are sourced from certified organic farms or regional growers following strict biodynamic agriculture guidelines. We verify soil testing and inspection certificates to maintain high organic quality.

Delivery Questions
Depending on your selection and routing area: Weekly Crates arrive every Tuesday afternoon, and Biweekly Crates arrive on alternate Thursdays. Exact operational dates and calendar timelines are fully visible in your dashboard.
Our crates are packed with organic thermal fiber-insulated liners and dry ice packs designed to keep milk and produce cold for up to 8 hours on your doorstep. You can specify a safe drop-off location or instructions in your address profile.
Yes. During national holidays or severe weather events, scheduling adjustments may occur. We will notify you via SMS and email at least 48 hours in advance of any routing shifts.
Our delivery boxes are 100% cardboard and recyclable. The thermal insulated liners are made of biodegradable natural cotton fibers and can be composted. The dry ice packaging film can be returned to soft plastic recycling points, or you can leave clean boxes on your doorstep for our driver to reuse.
Box Customization & Item Replacement
Yes. Every Wednesday morning, we publish the upcoming harvest inventory. You can swap crops you do not need, adjust quantities of items, and add pantry essentials from your dashboard. Customization options lock every Wednesday at midnight.
Since we harvest to order, dynamic field conditions can sometimes limit supply. If an item becomes unavailable, our packing crew will substitute it with a similar organic crop of equivalent value and quantity.
Yes, from your profile dashboard under allergen preferences, you can set exclusion tags (e.g., exclude nightshades, stone fruits, or gluten). Our harvest packer system will highlight warnings or swap items out automatically based on your list.
Subscription Pauses
Yes. You can skip individual delivery cycles or pause your entire subscription for longer intervals directly from the dashboard. Skip requests must be filed before the Wednesday midnight customization deadline to prevent billing.
Yes. Under your delivery scheduling dashboard, you can opt to route your upcoming box to our local food bank partners through our Community Harvest Program. This is a great alternative to pausing when you are out of town.
Billing & Cancellation Policy
Subscribers are billed automatically on a recurring basis after the Wednesday midnight lock window closes. We support credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex) and digital wallets. Invoices can be downloaded from your account panel.
You can cancel your subscription at any time without fees by visiting your account settings. To prevent being charged for the current week's box, cancellation must be processed before the Wednesday midnight lock deadline. Already processed orders cannot be canceled.
Refund Policy
If an order cannot be fulfilled or arrives with damaged ingredients, we will issue a credit or refund. Issues must be reported to our support team within 24 hours of delivery. Once verified, refunds are processed immediately and take 5-10 business days to appear on your payment method. Charges for successfully processed weekly boxes are non-refundable.
