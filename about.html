24-Hour Farm-to-Kitchen Loop

Our 24-Hour Freshness Guarantee

Supermarket produce sits inside wholesale shipping containers, storage warehouses, and display shelves for up to two weeks before reaching you. We redesigned the supply chain to bypass these storage nodes entirely.

1

Harvest On Demand

We harvest only the items matched to active subscriber numbers. No overproduction, no waste.

2

Thermal-Insulated Packing

Delivered inside breathable, climate-insulated cartons containing natural fiber liners to maintain temperature.

3

Direct Route Delivery

Crops travel in route-optimized, refrigerated trucks straight to your neighborhood route within hours.

Vetted Quality Checkpoints

Uncompromising Quality Standards

We employ dedicated inspectors at every stage of the supply cycle. From farm gate check-ins to dynamic customer feedback loop integrations, we ensure only premium produce is boxed.

01 / Farm Soil Audits

We inspect our partner farm soils regularly to ensure no runoff chemicals or synthetic compounds are present.

02 / Double Scan Inspection

Produce undergoes visual checks at the harvest site and a final grading process at our sorting hub.

03 / Biodegradable Liners

Our compostable mesh bags let roots breathe while protecting delicate greens from transit bruises.

04 / Box Rating System

Dynamic box review loops allow subscribers to rate specific crop batches, updating grower metrics instantly.
